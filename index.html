Grow

Seed

Plan by season

Quick seasonal pointers to schedule seeding, growth, and harvest.

Seed Early season

Cool-soil crops: lettuce, peas, carrots. Protect from late frost.

Grow Mid season

Warm-season care: tomatoes, corn. Stake, mulch, and irrigate evenly.

Harvest Late season

Pick at peak ripeness. Cure and store where appropriate.
